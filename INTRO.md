# INTRO — The Doctrine

*Read this if you read nothing else.*

## 1. The thesis

Extraction business models — auto-abo fleets, leasing returns, debt mills —
share one arithmetic: **the front-end price cannot cover the cost base.**
Average abo rate €563/month (Faaren Report 2024) against a documented cost
floor of ≈ €1,563+/month (ADAC Autokostendatenbank). The deficit is not a
bug; it is the engine. It makes the overcharge at contract end a structural
necessity, and structural necessity is *deterministic*: the opponent must make
the same mistakes every time.

So you don't hunt for weaknesses. You catalog the ways their P&L forces them
to fail, you verify the law that punishes each failure, and you wait.

**The SPOF is in their P&L, not in their case.**

## 2. The asymmetry

The opponent runs automation: template claims, template Gutachten, template
dunning. Automation is cheap, repetitive, and *cannot adapt* — a bot that has
seen one properly contested Mahnung has learned nothing. The defense can be
industrialized too, but it must be industrialized differently:

- **Cost per action ≈ 0.** A silent phase with a running § 548 clock is
  thermodynamically optimal: you expend zero energy while the opponent must
  continuously expend energy to keep the threat alive. You cannot do better
  than zero.
- **One flaw, two kill channels.** A claim calculated the way OLG Stuttgart
  6 U 84/24 forbids is *nicht plausibel dargelegt* — and a nicht plausibel
  dargelegte Forderung may not even be reported to SCHUFA (BGH VI ZR 375/24).
  The methodology defect poisons their lawsuit AND their data-edge threat
  simultaneously.
- **The judge is a player, not a referee.** Case-load pressure, anchoring,
  settlement pushes, pattern-matching, reactance — all modeled (J1–J5). The
  optimal court document is the one the judge can *plagiarize into the
  Entscheidungsgründe*: structure, density, brevity, zero rhetoric.

## 3. The epistemic rule (the one that changed everything)

The deepest vulnerability ever found in this system was not in the opponent's
matrix, the code, or the math. It was in the **citation supply chain** —
including two citations fabricated by the model itself, delivered with full
confidence, and absorbed into the knowledge base without resistance.

The fix is a meta-rule: **zero-trust must include the operator, the operator's
sources, and the model generating the engine.**

Operationally:
- **The statute carries the weight.** Cases corroborate. Never invert this.
- Every citation ships with a trust tier; below PROBABLE it is quarantined and
  structurally unemittable.
- Every proposition ships with a confidence in [0,1] — the engine can say
  "0.75 — arguable, not settled" and acts accordingly.
- The adjoint audit runs the weapon engine against *ourselves* before every
  maneuver: our evidence chain, our data hygiene, our damage estimates
  (ranges, never tariffs). *A weapon you cannot survive is not a weapon.*

## 4. The state machine

Disputes are thermodynamic systems. ACTIVE is gas: high entropy, high
dissipation. TUN is liquid. **CRYPTO is glass** — a minimum-energy
configuration that maintains structural integrity at zero metabolic cost.
Verjährung is a first-order phase transition: enforceability sits at ~1.0
until expiry, then drops discontinuously to zero, and the opponent's entire
sunk cost is absorbed by the phase boundary and recovered by no one.

So the default posture for a clock-backed dispute is **silence**, broken only
by named tripwires (Klage, SCHUFA threat, the 30-day tick, a settlement
offer), enforced by a fail-closed lock. Silence after a precision letter
carries ≈ 3.3 bits; silence as an opening move carries ~0. Sequence
determines information content.

## 5. What this engine will not do

- It will not file anything. Drafting is automated; *filing is human* — a
  lawyer reviews every court-facing document (§ 138 ZPO makes this survival,
  not courtesy).
- It will not claim certainty it does not have. Confidence < 1.0 everywhere
  except statute-anchored logic; the § 548-analog application to auto-abo is
  honestly priced at 0.75 and argued as an analogy chain, not asserted.
- It will not overclaim damages. Art. 82 DSGVO amounts are presented as
  ranges with citation support, because a tariff we invent is a methodology
  the opponent can attack — the adjoint principle again.
- It will not fight Pyrrhic battles. Five-state victory model: a win that
  costs more than it protects scores negative, and the engine recommends
  against its own "victories."

## 6. The definition, closed

> **"SPOF-free"** means: no weapon without CONFIRMED law under it; no
> confident output without a price on its confidence; no blind spot without
> a named cover; no victory definition that includes losing money; no move
> that spends the asymmetry.

The matrix doesn't have SPOFs anymore. We do — and now we know exactly where
they are. That is the only form "SPOF-free" can honestly take.
