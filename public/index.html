Doctrine

What makes it different

The statute carries the weight

Cases corroborate, never the inverse. Court documents emit CONFIRMED-tier law only; quarantined citations are structurally unreachable — they cannot be emitted, even by mistake.

Every proposition ships with a price

Confidence below 1.0 everywhere except statute-anchored logic. The auto-abo application of § 548 BGB is priced at 0.75 and argued as an analogy chain — not asserted as settled law.

The engine distrusts itself

Before every maneuver, an adjoint self-audit runs the weapon checks against its own position: overreach, data hygiene, tariffed damages. A weapon you cannot survive is not a weapon.

No two deployments share a fingerprint

Polymorphic generation permutes the surface while substance stays identical. Deterrence survives template capture — the arms race is priced in, not ignored.

Honesty

What this is — and what it is not

A defense engine that overclaims is an attack on its own user. The limits are architecture, not fine print.

L1

Not legal advice

It drafts. A qualified human lawyer reviews and files. That checkpoint is architectural, not a courtesy — § 138 ZPO makes truthfulness survival.

L2

The § 548 gap is priced, not hidden

Applying the six-month Mietrecht clock to auto-abo contracts is an argued analogy (confidence 0.75), not settled law. Its failure degrades the plan; it does not kill it.

L3

Legal knowledge has a shelf life

The citation registry is reverified on a calendar date — 2027-01-27. Until then it is verified bedrock; after, it is debt. The health endpoint exposes it as telemetry.

L4

Market data sets priors, nothing more

Deficit-structure evidence — subscription rates far below documented cost bases — steers strategy. It is never load-bearing in court.

L5

This demo is stateless

Demo runs are per-request and nothing is stored. The full API sits behind a fail-closed key gate; stateful operations live on the operator's own infrastructure.

L6

Rate-limited public surface

The demo endpoints throttle per client and cap inputs. The engine behind them is the production engine — no demo mode, no shortcuts.
